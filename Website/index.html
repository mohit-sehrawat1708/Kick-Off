The game

Choose a spot on the pitch.

  • 22 players on the pitch
  • About 30,000 fans in the stands
  • 23 PlayStyles

Eleven a side, full size

Two full teams on a 105 × 68 m pitch, two halves with ends changed, stamina that drains when you sprint, and five substitutions from the pause menu.

Take your man on

Skill moves on one button: a rainbow flick (a nutmeg if he’s tight), step-overs, ball rolls and roulettes. Shield it, close-control it, or knock it past him with an effort touch.

Pick your finish

Finesse, chip, power or low driven. Tap shoot again just before the strike for a timed finish, or fake the shot and walk it round the keeper.

Your team, your plan

Choose a formation, play style, opponent and starting XI. Change the team style and game plan in the middle of a match.

How to install
  1. Unzip the whole folder anywhere and keep the files together.
  2. Double-click PitchKings.exe. There is no installer, account or internet connection.
  3. If Windows says MSVCP140.dll is missing, run Engine\Extras\Redist\en-us\vc_redist.x64.exe once.
  4. If SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway. The game is not code-signed.
